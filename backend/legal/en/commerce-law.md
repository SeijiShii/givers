## Recurring Donations

- Recurring donations are charged automatically at the interval selected at signup (e.g., monthly) for the selected amount.
- The amount, billing interval, and next billing date of each charge are shown on the final confirmation screen at signup and in your account dashboard (My Page) after login.
- You may cancel at any time from your account dashboard (My Page). Cancellation stops all future charges; please complete it before the next billing date.
- Pausing and changing the amount are also available there.

## Cancellations and Refunds

- Donation refunds can be processed through the platform. Either the project owner or the donor may initiate a full refund, which is processed via Stripe. Refund processing may take several business days. Partial refunds are not supported.
- For recurring donations, cancellation stops all future charges. For charges already settled, refunds may be requested individually using the refund procedure above.
- If a payment error or duplicate charge occurs, please contact us through the contact form.
