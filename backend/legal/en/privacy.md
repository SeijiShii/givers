# Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 3, 2026

GIVErS ("the Service") respects your privacy and handles personal information appropriately. This policy explains what information the Service collects and how it is used.

## Article 1 (Information Collected)

The Service collects the following information.

**Account information**
- Email address, name (obtained via social login)

**Donation information**
- Donation project, amount, date, and refund status
- Anonymous donations are managed via browser tokens; no name or personal details are collected

**Contact information**
- Email address, name (optional), and message content entered in the contact form

**Usage information**
- Access logs (IP address, browser info, access date/time)

## Article 2 (Purpose of Use)

Collected information is used for the following purposes:

1. Providing, operating, and improving the Service
2. Managing donation history and notifying donors
3. Responding to inquiries
4. Preventing unauthorized use
5. Fulfilling legal obligations

## Article 3 (Disclosure to Third Parties)

The Service does not provide personal information to third parties except in the following cases:

- When the user has given consent
- When required by law
- When necessary to protect a person's life, body, or property

## Article 4 (Third-Party Services)

The Service uses the following third-party services:

**Stripe, Inc.**
Used for payment processing. Card information is not stored on the Service's servers; Stripe handles it securely. See [stripe.com/privacy](https://stripe.com/privacy) for Stripe's privacy policy.

**Google / GitHub / Apple (Authentication)**
Used for social login. Information is handled in accordance with each service's privacy policy.

## Article 5 (Cookies and Local Storage)

The Service uses cookies for session management. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may become unavailable.

## Article 6 (Data Retention)

- Account information: Donation records are retained in anonymized form even after account deletion.
- Contact information: Deleted approximately one year after the matter is resolved.
- Access logs: Retained for 90 days.

## Article 7 (User Rights)

Users have the right to:

- Request disclosure, correction, or deletion of their personal information
- Withdraw consent to data processing
- Request account deletion (donation records will be anonymized upon deletion)

To exercise these rights, please contact us via the contact form.

## Article 8 (Security)

The Service takes appropriate technical and administrative measures to protect personal information. However, complete security on the internet cannot be guaranteed.

## Article 9 (Changes to This Policy)

This policy may be revised without prior notice. We will notify users of significant changes through the Service.

## Contact

For questions or requests regarding the handling of personal information, please use the contact form.
