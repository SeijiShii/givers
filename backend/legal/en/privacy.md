## Article 1 (Information Collected)

The Service collects the following information.

**Account information**
- Email address, name (obtained via social login)

**Donation information**
- Donation project, amount, date, and refund status
- Anonymous donations are managed via browser tokens; no name or personal details are collected

**Contact information**
- Email address, name (optional), and message content entered in the contact form

**Usage information**
- Access logs (IP address, browser info, access date/time)

## Article 2 (Purpose of Use)

Collected information is used for the following purposes:

1. Providing, operating, and improving the Service
2. Managing donation history and notifying donors
3. Responding to inquiries
4. Preventing unauthorized use
5. Fulfilling legal obligations

## Article 3 (Disclosure to Third Parties and Outsourcing)

1. The Service does not provide personal information to third parties except in the following cases:
   - When the user has given consent
   - When required by law
   - When necessary to protect a person's life, body, or property
2. To the extent necessary to achieve the purposes of use, the operator may outsource payment processing, authentication, and similar functions to external service providers, or have such providers handle personal data (see Article 4). Some of these providers may handle personal data independently under their own responsibility.

## Article 4 (External Services and Cross-Border Transfers)

The Service uses the following external services, which may result in personal data being processed and stored outside Japan (primarily in the United States). The privacy policy of each provider applies. Personal data protection regimes in other countries may differ from those in Japan.

**Stripe, Inc. (United States)**
Used for payment processing (including Stripe Connect). Card information is not stored on the Service's servers; Stripe handles it securely and acts under its own responsibility with respect to payment processing. See [stripe.com/privacy](https://stripe.com/privacy) for Stripe's privacy policy.

**Google LLC / GitHub, Inc. (GitHub) / Discord Inc. (all United States, authentication)**
Used for social login. Information is handled in accordance with each service's privacy policy.

## Article 5 (Cookies and Local Storage)

The Service uses cookies and browser local storage for session management. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may become unavailable. The Service does not currently use third-party advertising or analytics tools (if introduced in the future, this will be disclosed in this policy).

## Article 6 (Data Retention)

- Account information: Donation records are retained in anonymized form even after account deletion.
- Contact information: Deleted approximately one year after the matter is resolved.
- Access logs: Retained for 90 days.

## Article 7 (Rights Regarding Retained Personal Data)

Users have the right to request notification of the purpose of use, disclosure, correction, addition, deletion, suspension of use, and suspension of provision to third parties of their retained personal data.

- Submit requests through the contact form or the contact above (quadii.shii@gmail.com). We will respond without delay in accordance with law after verifying your identity.
- There is, as a rule, no fee for such requests.
- If you request account deletion, donation records are retained in anonymized form after deletion.

## Article 8 (Security Measures)

The operator implements technical and organizational measures to prevent leakage, loss, or damage of personal information, including access control, encryption of communications, and non-retention of payment information (outsourced to Stripe). However, complete security on the internet cannot be guaranteed.

## Article 9 (Changes to This Policy)

If this policy is revised, significant changes will be announced on the Service, to the extent practicable, in advance of their effective date.
